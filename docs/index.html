Bitcoin Vault
Time-locked savings for your future

Create a digital savings vault that locks your Bitcoin until a specific date. Perfect for long-term savings, inheritance planning, or just keeping your funds safe from impulse spending.

The timelock is enforced by the Bitcoin network itself - not by any company or third party. No one can unlock your funds early.

How It Works

  • Create: Choose your unlock date
  • Deposit: Send BTC to the vault
  • Wait: Funds locked until target
  • Withdraw: Send anywhere after unlock

Your Security

  • 100% client-side - keys never leave your device
  • Download backup file - only way to access funds
  • No accounts, no servers, pure Bitcoin

Testnet4 is selected by default - learn without risk. Switch to Mainnet for real Bitcoin.
